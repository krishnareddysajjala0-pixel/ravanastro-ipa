Privacy Policy for Ravan Astro

Effective Date: November 10, 2026

Welcome to Ravan Astro ("we", "our", or "the App"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring your personal information is handled in a safe and responsible manner.

1. Information We Collect

To provide accurate Vedic astrological calculations and generate your Kundali (Horoscope), the App may ask you to input:

  • Name: To personalize your astrological chart.
  • Date of Birth and Time of Birth: Required for planetary position calculations and Lagna determination.
  • Place of Birth / Geographic Coordinates: Required for precise local ephemeris, sunrise/sunset, and planetary degree calculations.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Your birth information is used solely to calculate astrological data, planetary transits, and Kundali charts.
  • We do not sell, rent, trade, or share your personal data with any third-party advertisers.

3. Data Storage and Security

  • Your calculation data is processed securely and stored locally or on secure backend servers solely for generating your astrological reports.
  • We implement industry-standard security measures to safeguard your information against unauthorized access.

4. Third-Party Services

The App does not use any intrusive third-party ad networks or tracking SDKs that compromise your privacy.

5. Children's Privacy

Ravan Astro does not knowingly collect personal information directly from children under the age of 13 without parental consent. If parents or guardians enter information for horoscopes of their children, that information is handled with the same privacy protections.

6. Your Rights

You can clear or delete your saved astrological profiles at any time directly within the application.
